# Director Decision Policy — Golden Key Brand / Company

## Purpose

Choose, reject, degrade, or block a brand/company communication treatment from verified
organizational facts, brand thesis, desired audience association, identity system, evidence,
platform, source coverage, rights, cost, and live capability. This policy guides OpenMontage
canonical artifacts and append-only `decision_log`; it creates no parallel recipe.

## Resolved Context

Before planning, require resolvable references for:

- fact-only `CustomerFactSet` references for the communication object, company facts,
  user-stated desired association, identity facts, prohibited claims, spokesperson/mascot
  authorization, rights, and unknowns;
- no Golden Key-authored brand thesis, concept, duration, script, shot plan, audio treatment,
  runtime, or review conclusion. Derive the brand thesis and all creative treatment inside
  this OpenMontage Pipeline unless the customer explicitly supplied a direction;
- `platform_profile_ref` with the rules relevant to this task;
- versioned `project_content_context_ref` with frozen selected/resolved source revisions,
  exact document/image/temporal anchors, permissions, validity, conflicts, prohibited
  expressions, warnings, and gaps. It is evidence, never a Golden Key-authored brand thesis;
- task-independent material intake plus task-level `material_query_result`;
- audience, communication outcome, rights, unknowns, and any soft next step.

Ask one customer-facing fact or direction question at a time. Do not make customers choose a
technical Pipeline, provider, or renderer.
`selection_mode: none` forbids library retrieval. Every company fact, identity rule,
quotation, visible text, and visual choice cites eligible evidence inside the frozen project
scope; expired, conflicted, prohibited, non-referenceable, cross-project, or permission-
ineligible material is vetoed. Benchmark-only context remains benchmark evidence only.

## Decision Order (Binding)

Decide in this order: verified communication outcome and company facts; audience and
PlatformProfile; ideal brand narrative, identity, audio, and text treatment; duration; then
capability feasibility. Live availability may block execution, but it may not erase a
creatively warranted narrator, brand music motif, presenter, I2V, enhancement, or designed
composition. Preserve unavailable needs in `metadata.execution_requirements`.

## 1. Eligibility and Route-Out

Use this Pipeline when the dominant obligation is recognition, meaning, trust, reputation,
values, culture, or identity around a verified brand or organization.

| Dominant obligation | Route |
| --- | --- |
| sell or explain a specific product/service benefit | `golden-key-product-marketing` |
| build affection or recognition around a recurring person/animal/character | `golden-key-subject-ip` |
| qualify a lead around an offer and drive one direct action | `golden-key-lead-conversion` |
| extract moments from one long finished source | `clip-factory` |

A mascot or founder may appear here, but the organization remains the communication object.
If the person's recurring identity is the main promise, route to subject IP. A campaign with a
soft brand visit or follow action remains brand/company; a qualified inquiry with offer
mechanics belongs to lead conversion.

## 2. Brand Thesis and Evidence

Every concept must distinguish:

- `verified_company_fact`: supplied or visibly grounded organizational reality;
- `brand_thesis`: the approved meaning the piece wants audiences to remember;
- `desired_association`: the emotion, value, or category connection sought;
- `brand_evidence`: nearby people, place, process, document, product, behavior, or identity
  evidence that supports the thesis;
- `interpretation`: emotional or symbolic meaning, clearly separate from factual proof.

Veto invented history, founding story, ownership, scale, location count, staff qualification,
facility, process, culture, customer, partnership, award, credential, popularity, revenue,
impact, or result. Atmosphere may express a brand feeling but cannot prove capability.
Generated content cannot represent a real company event, employee, site, customer, or result.

## 3. Narrative Driver and Identity

Evaluate `emotional_motif`, `origin_or_mission`, `people_and_process`, `place_and_craft`,
`values_in_action`, `company_proof`, `founder_perspective`, `brand_character_story`, and
`graphic_manifesto`. Select one primary driver and name the evidence obligation it creates.

Resolve the identity system: approved logo variants, typography, colors, naming, tone,
spokesperson/mascot role, protected elements, forbidden modifications, and disclosure. A
mascot or emotional motif is a memory device, not proof of company capability.

Presenter modes are `none`, `real_original`, `real_rerecord`, `authorized_avatar`, or
`authorized_voice_clone`. A preset synthetic voice is narration, not a digital-human
presenter. Do not invent a founder quote or make an avatar impersonate an unapproved person.

## 4. Multi-Concept and Batch Rule

Generate at least three concepts that differ in thesis framing, emotional structure, evidence
order, source subset, narrator/presenter role, audio motif, visual identity expression, or
ending memory device—not merely wording, color, or transition style.

For multiple new videos, the OpenMontage Agent plans the portfolio and runs a separate
OpenMontage Project, canonical chain, and ProductionPackage per deliverable. Before the next
deliverable, read prior approved OpenMontage concepts and vary substantive dimensions. Golden
Key may pass prior artifact references but may not author a concept matrix or preselect the
differences. Do not pad quantity when verified evidence or source diversity is exhausted.

## 5. Audio, Text, Duration, and Ending

Resolve a finished audio architecture: primary meaning carrier, authorized voice path, brand
music motif and emotional curve, selected natural sound, restrained SFX, ducking, and
fallback. Music is part of brand memory when selected, not optional decoration.

Use `meta/text-layer-direction` to separate continuous captions, expressive emphasis,
titles/sections, annotations, brand line, and CTA. Record `text_layer_direction`; never force
all text into one subtitle style or apply a universal character count.

If duration is not supplied, compare at least two plausible bands using the thesis, evidence
beats, platform rhythm, speech feasibility, identity cadence, final brand-line readability,
and ending release. Append `duration_selection`; never select runtime from a template length.

The ending must complete the thesis through a deliberate memory device: a real action resolve,
brand line, identity reveal, motif return, or approved soft next step with visual and audio
clearance. A logo pasted onto an accidental hard stop is not a brand ending.

## 6. Source, Support, I2V, and Enhancement

Prefer real company evidence and approved identity assets. For every support layer, record the
specific communication gap it solves. I2V/enhancement may be `required`, `optional`, or
`rejected`; it may be rejected even when a benchmark mentions it.

Use generated support only when the named purpose, reference, protected elements, truth label,
prompt, negative prompt, sample gate, attempt budget, acceptance, fallback, and disclosure are
explicit. Never generate premises, employees, customers, historic scenes, certificates, or
operations and present them as real. Preserve logos, text, people, products, colors, and
identity anchors.

Every provided source selection carries exact path, source kind, context evidence/anchor,
revisions, role, and fallback. Temporal sources additionally carry exact `source_in`,
`source_out`, speed, and audio-analysis evidence; still images/documents carry exact region/
page/table/cell anchor, display duration, permission, and legibility/redaction treatment.
Source audio is retained, ducked, replaced, or muted per selected evidence; no blanket default applies.

## 7. Platform, Veto, and Scoring

Apply the PlatformProfile to hook, emotional value, proof style, pacing, narration, music,
text, framing, brand line, cover, disclosure, and review. A profile reference without visible
effect on relevant decisions fails.

Apply vetoes before scoring: wrong route, unsupported company fact, missing identity/likeness
rights, fabricated evidence, misleading generated realism, prohibited claim, or required
unavailable execution capability. Score survivors 0–5 on brand-thesis fit, audience relevance,
evidence coverage, identity distinctness, platform fit, source quality, capability feasibility,
cost, and risk. Creative merit and execution readiness remain separate.

## 8. Required Projection

Canonical artifact metadata and decisions must expose:

`communication_object`, `brand_thesis`, `desired_association`, `verified_company_facts`,
`brand_evidence_map`, `narrative_driver`, `identity_system`, `presenter_mode`,
`visual_sources`, `layout_strategy`, `text_layer_direction`, `audio_treatment`,
`source_support_policy`, `image_to_video_plan`, `platform_profile_ref`,
`execution_requirements`, and `review_obligations`.
