# Edit Director — Golden Key Brand / Company

Build the real-source anchor cut first from canonical shot-plan ranges. Verify the thesis is
understandable through actual people, place, process, product, or approved identity evidence
before adding graphics or generated support.

Use exact cuts and preserve the approved emotional/proof arc, identity cadence,
`render_runtime`, composition mode, audio architecture, brand claim boundary, and source truth.
Then add only approved narrator/source speech, brand music motif, natural sound, SFX,
captions, expressive layers, and support.

Record evidence-based `metadata.source_audio_policy` with mode, reason, volume, exact retained
ranges, source refs, and persisted analysis. Reject crew direction, counting, prompting,
unrelated speech, and unauthorized statements. No blanket mute or retain rule applies.

Record `metadata.text_layer_direction` and validate it. Continuous captions, emphasis,
titles, annotations, brand line, and CTA have different roles; overlays must not cover real
faces, products, processes, or company evidence.

Record `metadata.ending_treatment`: landing frame, final brand line, memory device, last
readable text time, visual release, audio tail, and why it resolves the thesis. A logo placed
on the export boundary is not closure; an intentional hard stop must be supported by the
approved concept and PlatformProfile.
