# Proposal Director — Golden Key Brand / Company

Read the policy, `meta/text-layer-direction`, `meta/taste-direction`, and
`meta/animation-runtime-selector`. Produce schema-valid `proposal_packet`, including a
task-specific `taste_profile`, and append `decision_log`. When atelier is a real candidate,
read `meta/bespoke-composition` before recommending composition mode. Use the ready material
snapshot and unranked initial coverage; read `meta/material-retrieval-boundary` and do not
reopen the whole library.

Resolve the frozen `ProjectContentContext` before concept comparison. Map each concept's
company facts, identity rules, quotations, document proof, image regions, and temporal proof
to exact context evidence IDs and anchors. Reject expired, unresolved-conflict, prohibited,
non-referenceable, cross-project, or permission-ineligible evidence. Confidential company
material may constrain direction but may not be quoted or shown publicly without public-use
permission. Empty context is valid and does not authorize library search. Concept-specific
queries stay inside frozen selected/resolved asset IDs.

Generate at least three structurally different concepts. Vary thesis framing, emotional arc,
company-evidence order, source subset, presenter/narrator role, audio motif, identity reveal,
or final memory device. A color, title, transition, or slogan swap does not count.

For each concept state:

- audience promise, first-three-second hook, brand thesis, and desired association;
- verified company facts and nearby evidence;
- role of people, places, processes, products, founder, mascot, or symbolic footage;
- narration/presenter, brand music motif, natural sound, captions, expressive text, brand line;
- identity-system treatment, support layers, I2V/enhancement decision, platform fit;
- runtime, composition mode, cost, rights/risk, fallback, and ending.

Veto unsupported facts, fabricated proof, identity/likeness violations, wrong route, and
misleading generated realism before scoring. I2V may be rejected. A benchmark request or tool
availability is not a creative reason.

Rank concepts first on thesis, evidence, audience, platform, identity, and truth. Select the
creative winner before applying the live capability snapshot. Then record execution
requirements and blockers without changing the winner merely because a local route is easier.
Only after that winner is recorded may the OpenMontage Agent author a concept-specific
`shot_candidates` request tied to the selected-concept decision. The result remains candidate
evidence and cannot choose final footage, order, exact ranges, audio, or generated support.

Resolve `render_runtime` and composition mode from live OpenMontage tool information. When
the `remotion` and `hyperframes` runtimes are both available, present both with task-specific
tradeoffs to the internal approval actor and append `render_runtime_selection`; never silently
default to either runtime. FFmpeg is valid only for an
approved simple source-led treatment. Use only manifest/registry tools in the production plan.

Resolve the full audio architecture and `text_layer_direction`, including the brand music
motif, voice authorization, natural-sound role, captions, emphasis, title, annotation, brand
line, and soft CTA. At proposal select the natural-sound role from persisted analysis; exact
ranges are decided at scene/edit. Every concept needs a definite evidence-backed ending.

For a batch request, every selected deliverable receives a separate OpenMontage Project,
canonical chain, and ProductionPackage; Golden Key does not pre-author a concept matrix.
