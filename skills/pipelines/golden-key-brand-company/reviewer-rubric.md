# Reviewer Rubric — Golden Key Brand / Company

Use with `meta/reviewer.md`. Treat the resolved PlatformProfile as a native review layer and
cite canonical artifact fields, source ranges, or timecodes. Do not build a second reviewer.

## Critical Findings

1. Wrong route: product/service sale, recurring-subject affinity, or qualified lead action dominates.
2. Brand thesis, company fact set, identity system, PlatformProfile, material query, rights, or unknowns are unresolved.
3. Invented history, ownership, scale, facilities, staff, culture, customer, award, credential, partnership, popularity, revenue, impact, or result.
4. Emotional atmosphere, mascot behavior, stock, graphics, or generated content is presented as company proof.
5. Logo, typography, color, name, person, place, product, process, or company truth drifts.
6. Concepts are cosmetic variants, or a batch repeats the same thesis/evidence/audio/ending structure.
7. Brand character or founder displaces the organization and silently turns the piece into subject IP.
8. Generated premises, company events, employees, customers, history, or operations are presented as real.
9. A provided shot lacks exact `source_path`, source kind, context anchor, evidence role, or fallback;
   or a temporal shot lacks exact `source_in`, `source_out`, and speed.
10. Runtime, provider, identity treatment, voice, music, support, brand line, or claim changes silently.
11. Approved narrator or brand music disappears because a provider is unavailable.
12. Text-role collapse merges captions, emphasis, titles, annotations, brand line, and CTA.
13. Duration has no `duration_selection` comparison when the user did not supply it.
14. The final logo/line is pasted onto an accidental stop without readable clearance, visual release, and audio tail.
15. A human-default gate advances without a matching approved `approval_policy` decision.
16. Source audio is retained without exact ranges and persisted analysis, overlaps crew direction, or is blanket-muted without a Director decision.
17. A generated or enhanced shot is used only because a benchmark or available tool asked for it; I2V may be rejected.
18. A required creative layer is removed because execution is unavailable instead of becoming an execution requirement/blocker.
19. Production-plan tools are not exposed by the manifest/registry, or the reasoning Agent is invented as an executable tool.
20. `scene_plan.metadata.shot_plan` omits scenes, lacks the applicable temporal range/speed or
    still/document anchor/display duration, contains gaps/overlaps, or violates the applicable
    timeline arithmetic.
21. Company proof is distant from the claim it supports, or symbolic interpretation is written as fact.
22. Publishing adds unsupported company claims or converts a recall piece into an unapproved direct-sales CTA.
23. ProjectContentContext ID/version/hash/project scope or frozen source revisions are
    unresolved; empty selection triggers retrieval; or cross-project/unselected/later-revision
    material enters the artifacts.
24. A company fact, quotation, identity rule, brand line, visible text, claim, or visual
    obligation lacks exact context evidence and asset/revision/page/table/region/time anchor,
    or uses expired, conflicted, prohibited, non-referenceable, permission-ineligible, or
    benchmark-only evidence as if it were a real customer fact.
25. A still image/document has invented source time, or lacks exact anchor, display duration,
    public-use permission, legibility/redaction, identity treatment, and fallback.

A ready material snapshot plus task-level `material_query_result` satisfies source understanding;
do not demand duplicate whole-library analysis. At proposal, exact retained audio ranges wait
until source shots are selected.

## Stage Review

- Idea: route, thesis, desired association, verified facts, identity, audience, platform,
  rights, duration reasoning, and context document/image/permission/conflict/expiry/gap effects.
- Proposal: at least three structurally different concepts; veto before score; evidence, audio motif, text, runtime, cost, fallback.
- Script: specific language, verified fact versus interpretation, coherent thesis-to-proof
  arc, no corporate filler, and exact permitted multimodal citations.
- Scene/assets: proof adjacency, exact temporal ranges or still/document anchors and display
  duration, identity preservation, provenance, permissions, legibility/redaction, prompts,
  disclosure, fallback.
- Edit/compose: anchor cut, emotional/proof rhythm, brand distinctness, finished audio, semantic text layers, last-second ending evidence.
- Publish: packaging matches the actual piece and adds no unsupported organizational claim.

## Content Performance Review

- one primary viewer payoff and at most one secondary payoff connect the opening relevance,
  company evidence, brand thesis, and memorable ending;
- suspense resolves and any discussion tension comes from a real brand choice or industry
  trade-off rather than manufactured opposition;
- the ending completes the thesis before at most one natural response action;
- title, one-focus cover, evidence-bounded copy, and relevant tags match the actual piece;
- early retention, average watch, completion, share, and interaction are diagnostic hypotheses,
  not an official ranking formula, semantic pass condition, or release gate.

Compare all artifacts and `decision_log` for stable refs, thesis, company facts, evidence,
identity, source lineage, audio motif, text roles, capability state, and ending. Schema-valid but
generic corporate montage fails semantic review.
The stable refs include ProjectContentContext, frozen source revisions, exact anchors,
permissions, validity, conflicts, prohibitions, benchmark labeling, and project isolation.
