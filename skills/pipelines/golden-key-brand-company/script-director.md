# Script Director — Golden Key Brand / Company

Read the approved brief, concept, decision log, company facts, identity rules, the
OpenMontage-authored selected-concept material query, `meta/material-retrieval-boundary`, and
PlatformProfile. Write the complete schema-valid spoken/text script, not an outline or a
sequence of corporate slogans. If the script creates a new evidence need, author a narrower
query with a script decision reference; Golden Key must not invent the requirement.

Resolve `ProjectContentContext` before writing. Every material-backed company fact,
quotation, name, identity rule, brand line, visible text, or visual obligation carries exact
`context_evidence_id`, asset/content/understanding revisions, and page/table/cell/region/temporal
anchor in section metadata. Public copy and imagery require public-use permission. Exclude
expired, unresolved-conflict, prohibited, non-referenceable, out-of-scope, and benchmark-as-
customer evidence. Surface a gap instead of filling it with corporate plausibility.

If the approved audio architecture uses generated narration, read
`meta/voice-performance-director` and persist top-level `voice_performance` plus section
delivery cues. Do not add narration merely because a TTS tool exists.

Each timed section records its brand-story function, spoken/source/expressive text, verified
fact or interpretation status, evidence/source reference, desired audience response, and
audio beat. Build one coherent thesis through emotional entry, real company evidence, and a
memorable landing. Keep mission, history, scale, culture, capability, partnership, customer,
and result claims strictly inside the supplied evidence.

## Viewer Response Architecture

Choose one primary viewer payoff and at most one secondary payoff from curiosity, resonance,
utility, conversation, trust, or offer. The opening should make the brand thesis relevant to a
specific audience through a real problem, human detail, credible contrast, or question; the
middle must deliver company evidence rather than slogans. Resolve every suspense setup. A
discussable viewpoint must come from an evidence-grounded brand choice or industry trade-off,
never manufactured opposition.

The ending closes the thesis and may invite at most one natural response; a memory line or
complete emotional landing is valid when interaction would weaken the piece. Early-retention
and average-watch targets are diagnostic hypotheses, not an official ranking formula and not
a reason to sacrifice context or credibility.

Voice must sound natural aloud and match the authorized speaker or narrator role. Music motif,
natural sound, captions, and expressive text should reinforce recognition without drowning
real people or evidence. The ending resolves the thesis; it does not append a generic sales CTA.
