# Asset Director — Golden Key Lead Conversion

Build schema-valid `asset_manifest` from approved scene/proposal artifacts. Every real asset
records exact source lineage, conversion role, claim boundary, rights, and current validity.
Every generated asset records provider/model when bound, prompt, negative prompt, reference,
protected elements, seed when available, cost, disclosure, sample approval, acceptance, and
fallback.

Verify offer, mechanism, proof, testimonial/case permission, qualification, legal copy,
contact destination, QR/link/handle, and CTA assets before execution. Generated support may
illustrate an abstract mechanism but may not fabricate customers, results, facilities,
credentials, demand, terms, eligibility, or contact information.

Resolve authorized presenter/narration/source speech, captions, music, natural sound, SFX,
evidence annotations, qualification text, and CTA as one system. Missing providers create an
execution blocker or approved same-promise fallback, never silent removal.

Resolve every `source_asset_ref` through material query/snapshot to an existing path, hash,
exact range, and audio-analysis evidence. Request a missing mapping once; never invent it.
Sample paid, identity-sensitive, testimonial-sensitive, or claim-sensitive generation before
batch execution.
