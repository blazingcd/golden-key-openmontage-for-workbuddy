# Compose Director — Golden Key Lead Conversion

Use the `edit_decisions.render_runtime` approved in `proposal_packet`; pass the proposal to
OpenMontage `video_compose` so runtime-swap detection stays active. Never silently replace
Remotion, HyperFrames, FFmpeg, provider, presenter, voice, music, proof, qualification, offer
term, or CTA. Route only to the locked `remotion`, `hyperframes`, or `ffmpeg` path; an
unavailable locked runtime is a blocker until an approved revised decision is appended.

Render only asset-manifest inputs and mix approved speech, music, natural sound, and SFX.
Verify existence, ffprobe, full decode, duration, resolution, frame rate, audio, wrapping/safe
zones, offer/people/logo/evidence accuracy, qualification/CTA readability, claim-proof
adjacency, contact destination, and generated disclosure.

Write schema-valid `render_report` and `final_review`. Inspect approximately
`duration-1.0s`, `duration-0.5s`, and `duration-0.1s`; listen through the tail and record
`final_review.checks.ending_closure`. The CTA and fit boundary must be readable, the next step
unambiguous, the visual release present, and audio untruncated.
