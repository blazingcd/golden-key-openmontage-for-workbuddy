# Director Decision Policy — Golden Key Lead Conversion

## Purpose

Choose, reject, degrade, or block a qualified lead-conversion treatment from a verified
audience, offer, mechanism, qualification boundary, proof, objections, compliance, platform,
source coverage, rights, cost, and live capability. This policy guides OpenMontage canonical
artifacts and append-only `decision_log`; it creates no parallel recipe.

## Resolved Context

Before planning, require resolvable references for:

- fact-only `CustomerFactSet` inputs. They may carry customer-supplied lead, offer,
  qualification, proof, compliance, rights, and CTA facts, but never a Golden Key-authored
  hook, concept, duration, script, shot plan, voice, music, runtime, or review conclusion;

- target lead and their relevant problem, aspiration, role, or situation;
- verified offer, deliverable, mechanism, scope, exclusions, terms, and current availability;
- qualification and disqualification conditions;
- claim/evidence map, allowed testimonial use, objections, compliance constraints;
- exactly one primary CTA and a verified contact/conversion path;
- `platform_profile_ref`, material snapshot/query, rights, and unknowns;
- versioned `project_content_context_ref` with frozen selected/resolved source revisions,
  exact document/image/temporal anchors, permissions, validity, conflicts, prohibited
  expressions, warnings, and gaps. It is evidence, never a conversion treatment.

Ask one customer-facing fact or direction question at a time. Technical choices belong to the
Director and internal gates, not the customer.
`selection_mode: none` forbids library retrieval. Every offer term, qualification, proof,
quotation, CTA/contact fact, visible text, and visual choice cites eligible evidence inside
the frozen project scope; expired, conflicted, prohibited, non-referenceable, cross-project,
or permission-ineligible material is vetoed. Benchmark-only context remains benchmark-only.

## Decision Order (Binding)

Decide in this order: verified lead objective, offer, qualification, evidence, compliance;
audience and PlatformProfile; ideal creative/audio/text treatment; duration; then capability
feasibility. Availability may block execution, but may not remove a warranted presenter,
authorized voice, music, proof visualization, I2V, enhancement, or CTA layer. Preserve
unavailable needs as `metadata.execution_requirements` with blocker/fallback status.

## 1. Eligibility and Route-Out

Use this Pipeline when the dominant obligation is to attract and qualify a lead around a
verified consultation, appointment, application, franchise/investment inquiry, channel,
recruitment, or collaboration offer and drive one direct next action.

| Dominant obligation | Route |
| --- | --- |
| awareness, consideration, trial, or purchase of a product/service | `golden-key-product-marketing` |
| recognition, meaning, or trust around a brand/company | `golden-key-brand-company` |
| recognition or affection around a recurring person/animal/character | `golden-key-subject-ip` |
| extract moments from one long finished source | `clip-factory` |

Soft save/follow/consideration remains product or brand work. Direct booking becomes lead
conversion when qualification, mechanism, proof, objection handling, contact path, or
appointment action dominates. An e-commerce purchase without lead qualification remains
product marketing.

## 2. Offer, Proof, and Qualification

Every concept must distinguish:

- `verified_offer`: what is actually provided and under what scope;
- `mechanism`: how the offer works, without implying unsupported causality;
- `qualified_lead`: who is and is not a plausible fit;
- `claim`: the factual or limited persuasive statement being made;
- `evidence`: nearby demonstration, document, authorized testimony, process, credential, or
  observable fact that supports the claim;
- `objection`: a real concern answered within verified facts;
- `primary_cta`: one concrete, current, trackable next action.

Veto invented price, discount, deadline, scarcity, quota, demand, revenue, ROI, payback,
earnings, guarantee, customer result, popularity, credential, approval, eligibility, location,
contact detail, or outcome. Atmosphere and attention footage do not prove offer quality.
Testimonials require authorization and support only the specific statements they contain.

## 3. Narrative Driver and Presenter

Evaluate `problem_mechanism_proof`, `aspiration_path_fit`, `qualified_invitation`,
`objection_answer`, `case_evidence`, `process_walkthrough`, `founder_or_expert_explanation`,
and `collaboration_opportunity`. Choose one primary driver; do not stack every persuasion
device into one short video.

Presenter modes are `none`, `real_original`, `real_rerecord`, `authorized_avatar`, or
`authorized_voice_clone`. A preset synthetic voice is narration, not a digital-human
presenter. Never fabricate a founder/expert/customer statement or use an avatar to create an
unmade endorsement.

## 4. Multi-Concept and Batch Rule

Generate at least three concepts that differ in relevance hook, mechanism explanation, proof
order, objection, qualification timing, source subset, meaning carrier, or CTA path—not merely
wording, urgency, or transition style.

For multiple new videos, the OpenMontage Agent plans the differentiated portfolio and runs a
separate OpenMontage Project, canonical chain, and ProductionPackage per deliverable. Before
the next video, read prior approved OpenMontage artifacts and vary substantive dimensions.
Golden Key may pass prior artifact references but may not write a concept matrix or pre-author
variants. Stop when proof or material diversity cannot support another honest video.

## 5. Audio, Text, Duration, and CTA

Resolve a finished audio architecture: meaning carrier, authorized voice, trust-building BGM
curve, selected natural sound, restrained SFX, ducking, and fallback. Do not use music or SFX
to manufacture urgency, scale, success, or emotional proof.

Use `meta/text-layer-direction` to separate continuous captions, expressive emphasis,
titles/sections, evidence annotations, qualification text, and CTA. Record
`text_layer_direction`; legal/qualification information must remain readable and may not be
hidden in fleeting fine print.

If duration is not supplied, compare at least two plausible bands using hook, mechanism,
proof, objection, qualification, speech feasibility, CTA readability, platform rhythm, and
ending clearance. Append `duration_selection`; local execution convenience is never a reason.

One video has one primary CTA. Record exact copy, destination/action, eligibility context,
tracking need, visual duration, spoken support, and fallback. The ending must give viewers
enough time to understand who should act, what happens next, and how—without fake urgency.

## 6. Source, Support, I2V, and Enhancement

Prefer real offer, process, people, place, product, document, and authorized evidence. For
every support layer, name the explanation or comprehension gap it solves. I2V/enhancement may
be `required`, `optional`, or `rejected`; a benchmark instruction is not a valid reason.

Generated support requires a named purpose, reference, protected elements, prompt, negative
prompt, sample gate, attempt budget, acceptance, fallback, disclosure, and truth label. It may
illustrate an abstract mechanism but may not fabricate a customer, result, testimonial,
facility, certificate, demand signal, contact, or offer term.

Every provided source selection carries exact path, source kind, context evidence/anchor,
revisions, claim/conversion role, and fallback. Temporal sources additionally carry exact
`source_in`, `source_out`, speed, and audio-analysis evidence; still images/documents carry
exact region/page/table/cell anchor, display duration, permission, and legibility/redaction
treatment. Source audio is retained, ducked, replaced, or muted per evidence; no blanket default applies.

## 7. Platform, Veto, and Scoring

Apply the PlatformProfile to relevance hook, proof density, pacing, voice, music, text,
qualification, CTA, cover, disclosure, prohibited claims, and review. Platform energy never
overrides truth, suitability, or readable offer terms.

Apply vetoes before scoring: wrong route, unverifiable offer, missing qualification, invented
proof, unapproved testimonial/likeness, prohibited claim, fake urgency, broken CTA, or required
unavailable execution capability. Score survivors 0–5 on qualified-audience relevance,
mechanism clarity, proof coverage, qualification honesty, objection value, CTA clarity,
platform fit, source quality, capability feasibility, cost, and risk. Keep creative merit and
execution readiness separate.

## 8. Required Projection

Canonical artifact metadata and decisions must expose:

`target_lead`, `verified_offer`, `audience_problem_or_aspiration`, `offer_mechanism`,
`qualification_conditions`, `claim_evidence_map`, `objection_strategy`, `primary_cta`,
`narrative_driver`, `presenter_mode`, `visual_sources`, `layout_strategy`,
`text_layer_direction`, `audio_treatment`, `source_support_policy`, `image_to_video_plan`,
`platform_profile_ref`, `execution_requirements`, and `review_obligations`.
