# Edit Director — Golden Key Lead Conversion

Build the real-source anchor cut first from canonical shot-plan ranges. Confirm the cut itself
communicates relevance, mechanism, proof, fit boundary, and one action before graphics or
generated support are added.

Use exact cuts and preserve approved `render_runtime`, composition mode, offer terms,
claim-proof adjacency, qualification, CTA, audio architecture, and source truth. Then add only
approved presenter/narration, music, natural sound, SFX, captions, evidence/qualification text,
expressive layers, and support.

Record evidence-based `metadata.source_audio_policy` with mode, reason, volume, exact retained
ranges, source refs, and persisted analysis. Reject crew direction, prompting, unrelated
speech, and unapproved statements. No blanket mute/retain rule applies.

Record and validate `metadata.text_layer_direction`. Continuous captions, emphasis, titles,
evidence annotation, qualification, and CTA have distinct roles. Qualification and contact
information must remain readable and cannot compete with decorative overlays.

Record `metadata.ending_treatment`: landing frame, CTA copy/action, qualification context,
last readable text time, visual release, audio tail, and why the viewer can understand the
next step. Accidental truncation or fake-urgency countdown fails; an intentional hard stop
requires concept and PlatformProfile support.
