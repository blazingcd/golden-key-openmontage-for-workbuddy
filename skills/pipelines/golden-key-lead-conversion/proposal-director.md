# Proposal Director — Golden Key Lead Conversion

Read the policy, `meta/text-layer-direction`, `meta/taste-direction`, and
`meta/animation-runtime-selector`. Produce schema-valid `proposal_packet`, including a
task-specific `taste_profile`, and append `decision_log`. When atelier is a real candidate,
read `meta/bespoke-composition` before recommending composition mode. Use the ready material
snapshot and unranked initial coverage; read `meta/material-retrieval-boundary` and do not
reopen the whole library.

Resolve the frozen `ProjectContentContext` before concept comparison. Map every concept's
offer, mechanism, qualification, objection, proof, CTA/contact, document, image, and temporal
evidence to exact context evidence IDs and anchors. Reject expired, unresolved-conflict,
prohibited, non-referenceable, cross-project, or permission-ineligible evidence. Confidential
facts may constrain the direction but may not be quoted or shown publicly without public-use
permission. Empty context is valid and does not authorize library search. Concept-specific
queries stay inside frozen selected/resolved asset IDs.

Generate at least three structurally different concepts. Vary relevance hook, problem versus
aspiration framing, mechanism explanation, proof order, objection, qualification timing,
source subset, presenter/narrator, emotional curve, or CTA path. Wording or urgency changes do
not count.

For each concept state:

- target lead, first-three-second relevance hook, and viewer promise;
- verified offer, mechanism, scope, qualification/disqualification, and one CTA;
- claim-evidence sequence, authorized testimonial/case use, and objection response;
- presenter/narration, trust-building music, natural sound, captions, emphasis, evidence/
  qualification annotation, and CTA treatment;
- support layers, I2V/enhancement decision, platform fit, runtime/composition mode, cost,
  compliance/risk, fallback, and ending.

Veto unverifiable offer terms, missing qualification, fabricated proof, unauthorized
testimonials/likeness, fake urgency, wrong route, and broken CTA before scoring. I2V may be
rejected. A benchmark or available tool is not a creative reason.

Rank concepts first on qualified-audience relevance, mechanism, proof, honesty, platform, and
CTA clarity. Select the creative winner before applying the live capability snapshot. Then
record execution requirements/blockers without changing the winner for local convenience.
Only after that winner is recorded may the OpenMontage Agent author a concept-specific
`shot_candidates` request tied to the selected-concept decision. Retrieval output remains
candidate evidence and cannot choose final footage, order, exact ranges, audio, or support.

Resolve `render_runtime` and composition mode using live OpenMontage tool info. When the
`remotion` and `hyperframes` runtimes are both available, present both with task-specific
tradeoffs to the internal approval actor and append `render_runtime_selection`; never silently
default to either runtime. Use only manifest/registry tools; the reasoning Agent is not an
executable tool.

Resolve the full audio architecture and `text_layer_direction`. Qualification and CTA text
must be readable, not fine-print decoration. Select the natural-sound role from persisted
analysis at proposal; exact ranges wait for scene/edit. Every concept needs a definite CTA
ending supported by existing or explicitly required assets.

For a batch request, every selected video receives a separate OpenMontage Project, canonical
chain, and ProductionPackage; Golden Key does not pre-author a concept matrix.
