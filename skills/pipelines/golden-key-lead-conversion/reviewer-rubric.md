# Reviewer Rubric — Golden Key Lead Conversion

Use with `meta/reviewer.md`. Treat the resolved PlatformProfile as a native review layer and
cite canonical artifact fields, source ranges, or timecodes. Do not build a second reviewer.

## Critical Findings

1. Wrong route: product consideration, brand recall, or recurring-subject affinity dominates.
2. Target lead, verified offer, mechanism, qualification, proof, objection, CTA, PlatformProfile, material query, rights, or compliance is unresolved.
3. Invented price, discount, deadline, scarcity, demand, revenue, ROI, earnings, guarantee, result, credential, approval, eligibility, contact, or offer term.
4. Attention footage, atmosphere, mascot/person behavior, stock, graphics, or generated content is presented as proof.
5. Testimonial, customer, founder, expert, partner, or result is fabricated, altered, decontextualized, or unauthorized.
6. Concepts are cosmetic variants, or a batch repeats the same hook/mechanism/proof/qualification/CTA structure.
7. The script asks for action before establishing relevance, mechanism, credible evidence, and fit boundary.
8. More than one competing primary CTA, or the CTA destination/action is unverified or unreadable.
9. A provided shot lacks exact `source_path`, source kind, context anchor, conversion role, or
   fallback; or a temporal shot lacks exact `source_in`, `source_out`, and speed.
10. Runtime, provider, presenter, voice, music, proof, qualification, offer, or CTA changes silently.
11. Approved presenter/narration/music disappears because a provider is unavailable.
12. Text-role collapse merges captions, emphasis, titles, evidence, qualification, and CTA.
13. Duration has no `duration_selection` comparison when the user did not supply it.
14. The ending truncates CTA, qualification, visual release, or audio tail, or uses fabricated urgency.
15. A human-default gate advances without a matching approved `approval_policy` decision.
16. Source audio is retained without exact ranges and persisted analysis, overlaps crew direction, or is blanket-muted without a Director decision.
17. I2V/enhancement is used only because a benchmark or available tool demanded it; it may be rejected.
18. A required creative layer is removed because execution is unavailable instead of becoming an execution requirement/blocker.
19. Production-plan tools are not exposed by the manifest/registry, or the reasoning Agent is invented as an executable tool.
20. `scene_plan.metadata.shot_plan` omits scenes, lacks the applicable temporal range/speed or
    still/document anchor/display duration, contains gaps/overlaps, or violates the applicable
    timeline arithmetic.
21. Qualification is vague, hidden, discriminatory without legitimate basis, or contradicted by broad CTA copy.
22. Publishing adds unsupported offer terms or changes the approved action/contact path.
23. ProjectContentContext ID/version/hash/project scope or frozen source revisions are
    unresolved; empty selection triggers retrieval; or cross-project/unselected/later-revision
    material enters the artifacts.
24. An offer term, qualification, quotation, contact/CTA detail, visible text, claim, or
    visual obligation lacks exact context evidence and asset/revision/page/table/region/time
    anchor, or uses expired, conflicted, prohibited, non-referenceable, permission-ineligible,
    or benchmark-only evidence as if it were a real customer fact.
25. A still image/document has invented source time, or lacks exact anchor, display duration,
    public-use permission, legibility/redaction, conversion role, and fallback.

A ready material snapshot plus task-level `material_query_result` satisfies source understanding;
do not demand duplicate whole-library analysis. At proposal, exact retained audio ranges wait
until source shots are selected.

## Stage Review

- Idea: route, lead, offer, mechanism, qualification, proof, CTA, compliance, rights,
  duration reasoning, and context document/image/permission/conflict/expiry/gap effects.
- Proposal: at least three structurally different concepts; veto before score; objection, audio/text, runtime, cost, fallback.
- Script: specific offer copy, relevance, mechanism, nearby proof, fit boundary, one CTA, no
  fake urgency, and exact permitted multimodal citations.
- Scene/assets: exact temporal ranges or still/document anchors and display duration, claim-
  proof adjacency, current offer/contact assets, permissions, legibility/redaction, prompts,
  disclosure, fallback.
- Edit/compose: anchor cut, information hierarchy, readable qualification/CTA, finished audio, semantic text layers, last-second evidence.
- Publish: packaging matches the actual video, offer, qualification, and conversion action.

## Content Performance Review

- one primary viewer payoff and at most one secondary payoff connect the opening relevance,
  verified mechanism/proof, qualification, and one CTA;
- suspense resolves and no fear, false urgency, conflict, or polarisation is manufactured for
  retention or comments;
- the ending completes the argument and gives exactly one approved action enough time;
- title, one-focus cover, evidence-bounded copy, and relevant tags match the offer and video;
- early retention, average watch, completion, and conversion are diagnostic hypotheses, not
  an official ranking formula, semantic pass condition, or release gate.

Compare all artifacts and `decision_log` for stable refs, offer terms, lead, proof,
qualification, objections, source lineage, audio, text roles, capability state, and CTA. A
schema-valid generic hard-sell advertisement fails semantic review.
The stable refs include ProjectContentContext, frozen source revisions, exact anchors,
permissions, validity, conflicts, prohibitions, benchmark labeling, and project isolation.
