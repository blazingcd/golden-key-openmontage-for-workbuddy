# Director Decision Policy — Golden Key Product Marketing

## Purpose

Choose, reject, degrade, or block a product/service marketing treatment from verified facts,
audience need, evidence, platform, source coverage, rights, cost, and live capability. This
policy guides existing canonical artifacts and `decision_log`; it creates no parallel recipe.

## Resolved Context

Do not plan until the control plane provides resolvable references for:

- fact-only `CustomerFactSet` references for the marketed object, audience, rights,
  user-stated action, and explicit unknowns. Golden Key must not supply the value proposition,
  concept, duration, script, shot plan, voice, music, runtime, or review conclusion;
- `platform_profile_ref` with applied rules;
- versioned `project_content_context_ref` with frozen selected/resolved source revisions,
  exact document/image/temporal anchors, permissions, validity, conflicts, prohibitions,
  warnings, and gaps. It is evidence, never a value proposition or creative treatment;
- task-independent material intake plus task-level material query;
- user goal, audience situation, CTA intensity, rights, and unknowns. The live capability
  snapshot enters at proposal, after idea has selected the ideal creative treatment.

Ask customers one fact or high-impact direction question at a time. Technical decisions stay
with Director and internal gates.
`selection_mode: none` forbids library retrieval. Every context-backed fact, benefit basis,
quotation, visible text, and visual decision cites an eligible in-project evidence anchor;
expired, conflicted, prohibited, non-referenceable, cross-project, or permission-ineligible
material is vetoed.

## Decision Order (Binding)

For every concept decide in this order: verified goal/evidence, platform/audience, ideal
creative and audio treatment, duration, then capability feasibility. The capability snapshot
decides whether the approved plan can execute now. It must not erase a creatively warranted
voice, music, presenter, generated support, or enhancement layer. Preserve unavailable
requirements as explicit execution requirements; block before execution unless an
approved fallback delivers the same promise for a genuine creative reason.

## 1. Eligibility and Route-Out

Use this Pipeline for awareness, consideration, trial, or purchase of a verified product,
digital product, service, or packaged offer.

| Dominant obligation | Route |
| --- | --- |
| remember, like, or trust a recurring person/animal/character | `golden-key-subject-ip` |
| prove company identity, history, capability, or values | `golden-key-brand-company` |
| qualify leads around an offer and drive one direct conversion action | `golden-key-lead-conversion` |
| extract multiple moments from one long finished source | `clip-factory` |

Soft service seeding and save/consideration CTAs remain product marketing. Route to lead
conversion only when qualification, offer mechanics, objection handling, or direct booking
dominates.

## 2. Fact to Benefit to Evidence

Every concept must distinguish:

- `fact`: supplied or visibly verified product/service reality;
- `benefit`: what that fact plausibly means for the intended audience;
- `evidence`: nearby visual, spoken, documented, or demonstrated support;
- `impression`: atmosphere or subjective invitation that is not stated as an effect claim.

Veto unsupported efficacy, before/after, price, offer, scarcity, review, credential, address,
popularity, revenue, or result claims. For beauty and wellness services, relaxation imagery
does not prove medical, therapeutic, scalp, hair, sleep, pain, or shoulder/neck outcomes.

## 3. Choose Narrative Driver and Presenter

Evaluate `demonstration`, `use_journey`, `sensory_experience`, `problem_solution`,
`comparison`, `testimonial`, `presenter_explanation`, `narrated_source_story`, and
`graphic_explanation`. Choose one primary driver and use support only for named gaps.

Presenter modes are `none`, `real_original`, `real_rerecord`, `authorized_avatar`, or
`authorized_voice_clone`. A preset AI voice without an on-screen avatar is narration, not a
digital-human presenter. Never add a presenter because the tool exists.

## 4. Multi-Concept and Batch Rule

For one deliverable, generate at least three concepts that differ in structure, hook,
evidence order, source subset, meaning carrier, or CTA—not merely wording.

For multiple requested new videos, the OpenMontage Agent plans the differentiated portfolio
and runs a separate OpenMontage Project, canonical artifact chain, and ProductionPackage per
selected deliverable, serially or as isolated jobs. Before directing the next deliverable,
read prior approved OpenMontage concepts and deliberately vary substantive dimensions. Golden
Key may create job identities and pass prior OpenMontage artifact references, but it may not
write a concept matrix, choose the differences, or pre-author variants. Do not use `clip-factory`
unless the source is long-form content being extracted. Do not pad quantity when evidence or
material diversity declines.

Batch diversity is judged across the portfolio: primary driver, hook, evidence sequence,
source subset, audio carrier, duration rationale, emotional curve, and CTA may not all repeat.
Each deliverable still makes its own decisions rather than inheriting a prewritten variant.

## 5. Audio and Text

Resolve the finished audio architecture: meaning carrier, voice path when used, BGM decision
and emotional curve, natural-sound moments, SFX restraint, ducking, and fallback. Missing
providers must not silently erase an approved audio layer.

If duration is not supplied, compare at least two plausible bands using hook, evidence count,
speech feasibility, source variety, platform rhythm, CTA clearance, and ending. Record a
`duration_selection` decision. Do not select duration from a renderer template or because a
shorter local cut is easier to execute.

Choose text by content role before typography using `meta/text-layer-direction`: continuous
captions, expressive emphasis, title/section, CTA, and annotation are distinct. Character
counts remain task-specific.

## 6. Source, Support, I2V, and Enhancement

For each support layer, record the problem it solves. Existing source is preferred when it
already communicates the selected promise.

I2V/enhancement may be `required`, `optional`, or `rejected`. Use it only when:

1. motion or restoration has a named purpose;
2. reference and protected elements are sufficient;
3. provider limits can protect people, product/service facts, logo, text, color, and action;
4. generated motion is not presented as real evidence;
5. prompt, negative prompt, sample gate, attempt budget, acceptance, and fallback are explicit.

A benchmark instruction that merely demands one AI shot is not a valid Director reason.

## 7. Platform and Candidate Scoring

Apply PlatformProfile rules to promise, hook, proof style, pacing, voice, music, text,
framing, CTA, cover, and review. A profile reference with no applied rules fails.

Apply vetoes first: wrong route, unsupported fact, missing rights, product/service distortion,
evidence fabrication, required unavailable capability, or prohibited claim. Score survivors
0–5 on goal fit, audience relevance, evidence coverage, platform fit, source quality,
creative distinction, capability feasibility, cost, and risk. Record selected/rejected
reasons and approved fallbacks in `decision_log`.

For clarity, an unavailable required capability vetoes immediate execution, not Director
consideration or selection. Report creative merit and execution readiness separately.

## 8. Required Projection

Existing artifact metadata and decisions must expose:

`marketed_object`, `audience_problem_or_desire`, `value_proposition`,
`claim_evidence_map`, `narrative_driver`, `presenter_mode`, `visual_sources`,
`layout_strategy`, `text_layer_direction`, `audio_treatment`, `source_support_policy`,
`image_to_video_plan`, `platform_profile_ref`, `execution_requirements`, and
`review_obligations`.
