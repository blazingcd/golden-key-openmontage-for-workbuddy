# Proposal Director — Golden Key Product Marketing

Read the decision policy, `meta/text-layer-direction`, `meta/taste-direction`, and
`meta/animation-runtime-selector`. Produce schema-valid `proposal_packet`, including a
task-specific `taste_profile`, and append `decision_log`. When atelier is a real candidate,
read `meta/bespoke-composition` before recommending composition mode.
Read `meta/material-retrieval-boundary` before using any snapshot or query result.

A ready Golden Key `MaterialIndexSnapshot`, complete-ingest receipt, and task-level
`material_query_result` are this product's Source Understanding Review: technical probes,
reusable ranges, visual/audio semantics, timestamped speech, and provenance were persisted at
upload time. The Handoff result is unranked initial coverage, not a creative shortlist. Do not
request another whole-library `source_media_review` or reopen all files.

Resolve the frozen `ProjectContentContext` before concept comparison. For each concept map
its material-backed fact-benefit-evidence chain to exact context evidence IDs and document
page/table/cell, image region, or temporal anchors. Reject expired, unresolved-conflict,
prohibited, non-referenceable, cross-project, or permission-ineligible material. Confidential
facts may constrain the plan but may not become spoken/visible/public claims without
public-use permission. An empty context is a valid no-material condition, not authority to search.

Generate at least three genuinely different concepts. Vary narrative structure, hook,
evidence order, source subset/hero moment, meaning carrier, emotional curve, or CTA. A title
or wording swap does not count. Veto unsupported facts, wrong route, missing rights,
fabricated proof, product/service distortion, and required unavailable capability before
scoring.

For each concept state audience promise, first-three-second hook, marketed-object visibility,
fact-benefit-evidence chain, source evidence, audio treatment, text roles, support layers,
I2V/enhancement decision, platform fit, cost, risk, and fallback. I2V may be rejected; a
benchmark request is not a creative reason.

Observable atmosphere or service actions prove only what is actually visible. They do not
prove efficacy, popularity, customer satisfaction, staff credentials, price value, or results.
Reject any concept whose voice, identity, review, or source claim lacks rights. Give every
concept a definite evidence-backed ending rather than "if available" footage.

Resolve renderer family, `render_runtime`, and composition mode using current tool info, then
append a `render_runtime_selection` decision. When `remotion` and `hyperframes` are both
viable, present both to the internal approval actor with brief-specific tradeoffs and record
the approved value; customers see the natural-language recommendation rather than a technical
runtime question. FFmpeg is only valid for a deliberately simple source-led treatment.

Carry `platform_profile_ref`, its version/hash, and `actual_target_platform` into
`proposal_packet.metadata`. Rank concepts first on creative,
evidence, platform, truth, rights, and product preservation. Apply live capability status
after selection to decide execution readiness, demands, and blockers; availability may not
change the creative winner.
After that winner is recorded, the OpenMontage Agent authors the concept-specific
`shot_candidates` request with a decision reference. Golden Key only executes retrieval; the
result cannot decide hero footage, order, exact ranges, audio, or generated support.
Restrict every selected-concept query to the context's frozen selected/resolved asset IDs;
similarly named products and later library revisions remain out of scope.
The selected creative rationale must not praise avoiding unavailable providers, local
convenience, lower execution risk, or lower cost. Record those only in the post-selection
capability/cost audit.

Populate `production_plan.stages[].tools` only with tools exposed by the active OpenMontage
manifest or tool registry. Planning stages may have an empty tool list; the OpenMontage
Agent is not an executable tool and must not be represented as a production-plan tool or an
invented generator/planner/provider. Record unavailable needs in
`metadata.execution_requirements`, including purpose, affected stage, availability,
fallback, and blocker status. The Pipeline chooses tools; Golden Key supplies capability
facts and execution policy but does not prescribe a production recipe.

Preserve unavailable narration, source-audio, generation, and composition requirements as
blockers rather than claiming manual FFmpeg can provide an unsupported function. Omit unused
optional voice fields instead of null, classify any selected CTA as a CTA text role, and keep
voice choice grounded in identity, consent, tone, and creative fit rather than availability.

Resolve meaning carrier, voice path, BGM source/curve, natural sound, SFX, ducking, caption
role, expressive text, CTA treatment, and provider/cost status. Do not leave finished audio
as “optional.” Populate the existing production plan and metadata; do not create a recipe
artifact. For a batch request, each selected deliverable later receives its own chain and
ProductionPackage.

At proposal, choose the natural-sound role and cite the persisted audio-analysis source. Lock
exact retained ranges only at scene/edit after source shots are selected.
