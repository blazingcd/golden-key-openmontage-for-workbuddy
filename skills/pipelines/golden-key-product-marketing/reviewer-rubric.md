# Reviewer Rubric — Golden Key Product Marketing

Use with `meta/reviewer.md`.

Treat the resolved PlatformProfile as one of the native review layers in
`meta/reviewer.md`. Review the decisions relevant to the current stage and cite concrete
artifact fields, source ranges, or timecodes. Do not create a second Golden Key reviewer or
require a mechanical echo of every platform rule ID.

## Critical Findings

1. Wrong route: the real objective is subject IP, company/brand proof, or qualified lead conversion.
2. Unresolved fact set, PlatformProfile, task-level material query, capability snapshot, or rights.
3. Unsupported product/service effect, price, offer, review, credential, address, scarcity, popularity, or result.
4. Benefit has no nearby evidence, or atmosphere is presented as efficacy proof.
5. Product, logo, text, person, service action, or source truth drifts.
6. Concepts are cosmetic variants, or a batch repeats the same video with surface changes.
7. Generated/stock/graphic content is presented as real product or service evidence.
8. I2V/enhancement is used only because a benchmark or available tool demanded it.
9. A source shot lacks exact `source_path`, source kind, context anchor, role, or fallback; or a
   temporal shot lacks exact `source_in`, `source_out`, and speed.
10. Runtime, provider, voice, music, support, CTA, or claim changes silently.
11. Approved narration/music disappears because a provider is unavailable.
12. Text-role collapse merges captions, emphasis, titles, annotations, and CTA into one layout.
13. The ending is only a timeline boundary: the last picture, CTA/caption, BGM, or natural sound
    stops without an intentional landing beat, readable clearance, or reviewed audio tail.
14. Capability-driven creative collapse: the artifact omits a creatively warranted narration,
    music, presenter, I2V, enhancement, or other layer because the provider/executor is not
    currently available, instead of preserving it as an explicit execution requirement and
    execution blocker. Capability status may be documented at proposal after selection when
    an independent creative, truth, rights, identity, or customer-governance reason already
    rejects an option and availability did not change the selected direction.
15. Unjustified duration: the customer did not supply duration and no `duration_selection`
    decision compares at least two plausible bands on evidence, platform, audio, CTA, and
    ending needs, or local execution convenience is the stated reason.
16. A human-default planning checkpoint is auto-advanced without an `approval_policy`
    decision matching the supplied actor, scope, reference, constraints, and exclusions, or
    its matching entry does not set `user_approved: true`.
17. At scene, asset, or edit stage, source audio is retained without exact source ranges and persisted audio-analysis
    evidence, or a retained range overlaps crew direction, counting, prompting, or unrelated
    speech. A blanket default-mute decision also fails; the Director must decide from the
    selected evidence and concept.
18. Observable atmosphere or service action is inflated into efficacy, popularity, customer
    satisfaction, staff qualification, value-for-money, or another unsupported claim.
19. Production-plan tools are not exposed by the active OpenMontage manifest/registry, the
    final compose tool contradicts the locked runtime, an unused optional field is null, CTA
    copy has no CTA text role, or an ending depends on unretrieved conditional footage.
20. A stage invents a generator/planner/provider, treats the reasoning Agent as an executable
    tool, or hand-waves unavailable narration, source audio, generation, or composition as
    manual FFmpeg without an actual supported path.
21. A creatively preferred authorized voice is replaced by preset TTS only because the former
    is unavailable, instead of preserving the ideal demand and execution blocker.
22. `scene_plan.metadata.shot_plan` omits a scene, hides executable timing only in prose,
    lacks an exact temporal range/speed or still/document anchor/display duration, disagrees
    with its scene boundaries, leaves an unintended main-timeline gap/overlap, or violates
    the applicable temporal-range or display-duration arithmetic.
23. ProjectContentContext ID/version/hash/project scope or frozen source revisions are
    unresolved; empty selection triggers retrieval; or an artifact uses a cross-project,
    unselected, or later-revision asset.
24. A material-backed fact, price, parameter, quotation, visible label, claim, or visual
    obligation lacks an exact context evidence and asset/revision/page/table/region/time
    citation, or uses expired, conflicted, prohibited, non-referenceable, or permission-
    ineligible evidence.
25. A still image/document is given invented temporal ranges, or a document/image shot lacks
    exact anchor, display duration, public-use permission, legibility/redaction, and fallback.

A ready Golden Key `MaterialIndexSnapshot` with complete-ingest receipt and task-level
`material_query_result` satisfies Source Understanding Review. Do not require duplicate
whole-library `source_media_review`. At proposal, exact retained audio ranges are deferred
until source shots are selected.

Do not require an out-of-schema platform enum. `target_platform: generic` is valid for
Xiaohongshu, Douyin, Kuaishou, or WeChat Channels only when
`metadata.actual_target_platform` and the resolved PlatformProfile consistently identify the
real platform and the artifact visibly applies its relevant constraints.

## Stage Review

- Idea: eligibility, marketed object, audience, facts, unknowns, platform, source reality,
  editorial duration reasoning, creative-before-capability ordering, and explicit execution
  blockers for unavailable requirements; context documents/images/permissions/conflicts/
  expiry/gaps affect the brief without supplying creative answers.
- Proposal: 3+ structurally different concepts; veto before score; complete audio/text/I2V/cost/fallback.
- Script: specific spoken copy; fact-benefit-evidence or clearly subjective impression; one
  main promise; exact permitted multimodal citations for every context-backed line.
- Scene/assets: proof adjacency; exact temporal ranges or still/document anchors and display
  duration; provenance; permissions; legibility/redaction; prompts, protection, acceptance, fallback.
- Edit/compose: anchor cut works; pacing and text hierarchy support evidence; finished audio; no silent substitution;
  the final second has dedicated temporal evidence and the ending closes the selected promise.
- Publish: packaging matches actual video and adds no unsupported fact.

## Content Performance Review

- one primary viewer payoff and at most one secondary payoff connect the opening promise,
  source-backed product/service evidence, and ending;
- every suspense setup resolves and no dissatisfaction, review, scarcity, conflict, or
  efficacy claim is manufactured for retention or comments;
- the ending completes the content before at most one funnel-appropriate action;
- title, one-focus cover, evidence-bounded copy, and relevant tags match the actual video;
- early retention, average watch, completion, save, and interaction are diagnostic hypotheses,
  not an official ranking formula, semantic pass condition, or release gate.

Compare every canonical artifact and `decision_log` for stable refs, selected concept,
claims/evidence, source lineage, audio, text roles, capability state, and fallback. Schema-valid
but generic product advertising fails semantic review.
The stable refs include ProjectContentContext, frozen source revisions, exact anchors,
permissions, validity, conflicts, prohibitions, and project-source isolation.
